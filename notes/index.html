在期末周的混乱里，重新建立秩序Rebuilding order amid finals-week chaos

课程、科研、时间管理同时向前推进时，最先需要修复的往往不是效率，而是对下一步的判断。

When coursework, research, and time management all move at once, the first thing to repair is often judgement, not efficiency.

我开始把一周拆成可以交付的小块：一个可运行的基线，一张解释结果的图，一段能让别人复现的记录。

秩序不是把所有事情排得整整齐齐，而是知道今天结束时什么会真正留下来。

I started breaking a week into small deliverables: a runnable baseline, one explanatory figure, and a record someone else can reproduce.

Order is not about making every task look neat. It is about knowing what will still exist at the end of the day.
